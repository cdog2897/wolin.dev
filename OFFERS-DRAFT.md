The extension does not enroll the client in a paid monthly subscription.

**Post counting:** Quantities now count publications across the three platforms, rather than unique original assets. The 90-day offer provides 60 platform posts per month, or 180 over three monthly periods. The creative mix and reuse/adaptation across platforms still need production planning.

## Draft operating and pricing rules

- **One-time offers:** Propose full payment before work starts for these small fixed projects. Work begins after payment, required access, and complete materials. Filming/photography delivery time starts after the session.
- **Monthly offers:** Propose payment before each service month. The social subscriptions are month to month, as confirmed by Caleb. Website care follows the existing package terms. For new monthly agreements, propose cancellation notice before the next billing date, with service through the paid period; finalize this wording before agreements are created. Terms for recurring custom-quote work are agreed in the quote.
- **Revisions:** One consolidated round unless a listing states otherwise. Agree on additional work and its price before starting it.
- **Outside costs:** Advertising, new premium software, specialist vendors, and travel outside the agreed area require a separate approved budget. Existing website care already includes its operating tools and covered domain costs.
- **Custom quotes:** Other services have no published prices or standard package quantities. Define the work and fee after reviewing the business’s needs.
- **Avoid duplicate scope:** Shared audits, account setup, copy, schema, content assets, tracking, and reports should appear once in a combined quote.
- **Results:** The 90-day offer guarantees 10,000 combined views across Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok in 90 days, with continued management at no extra charge until reached if the threshold is missed. Reporting details and eligibility still need agreement language. Monthly packages have no guarantee.
- **Capacity:** Confirm delivery capacity across 90-day social programs, monthly social subscriptions, website builds, and custom projects before deciding how much work to sell.
